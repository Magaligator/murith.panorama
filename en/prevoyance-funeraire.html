Funeral pre-planning

Plan your own funeral in advance

We rarely think about it, but preparing your own funeral in advance is one of the most precious gifts you can give to your loved ones. It spares them from making difficult decisions in the pain of grief.

At Murith funeral services, you can organise every detail of your funeral in advance. The type of ceremony you want, secular or religious. The music you love. The flowers you prefer. The place of burial. Your last wishes are recorded and kept in a safe place.

Your loved ones will no longer have to wonder what you would have wanted. Everything is already decided, according to your wishes, with your agreement.

Trust above all

Why choose Murith funeral services for your funeral pre-planning?

A pre-plan may be prepared 5, 10, 20 or even 30 years ahead. Beyond the financial side, it is about choosing the people to whom you will entrust the organisation of your last wishes.

A history since 1888

In Geneva, Murith funeral services have been supporting families since 1888: soon 140 years of experience and trust, with the same mission – supporting every family with dignity, respect and professionalism.

A complete service

Secular or religious ceremony, funeral rooms open 24/7, the Cluse chapel, coffins, flowers, international transport and repatriation, administrative procedures: a single team supports you, from the first hour to the final tribute.

A team that knows the field

Several organisations offer pre-planning solutions, with independent foundations, insurers or partners in charge of organising the funeral. At Murith, you know exactly who will be there when the time comes: we master the administrative procedures, the ceremonies and families' expectations. Murith also trains the funeral directors of tomorrow throughout French-speaking Switzerland, with the Swiss Association of Funeral Services (SVB), notably for the Federal Diploma: a guarantee of reliability and clarity for funerals carried out to the highest professional standards (the only private company in Geneva holding the Federal Diploma).

Because every life is unique, every pre-plan must be too: we listen to your wishes to prepare an organisation that reflects you.
