Understanding the cost of a funeral in Geneva: advice and cost estimate

Table of contents:

  1. Administrative procedures and their costs
  2. Burial or cremation
  3. Breaking down the cost of a funeral in Geneva
  4. Indicative price guide
  5. Floral arrangements for a funeral in Geneva
  6. Funeral rooms
  7. In summary: the cost of a funeral in Geneva

How much does a funeral cost? This question often weighs on families at the most difficult times. As a general rule, in Switzerland, you should expect costs of 3,000 to 10,000 CHF. This article gives an overview of the essential expenses, the optional choices that can affect your budget, and recommendations for balancing respect for final wishes with a controlled budget.

1. Administrative procedures and their costs

In Geneva, several administrative steps follow a death: issuing the medical death certificate, declaring the death to the civil registry and obtaining the death certificate. They are governed by cantonal regulations. For details, see our page on death formalities.

When you entrust the funeral to Murith funeral services, all death formalities are taken care of, to ease your burden at a difficult time.

2. Burial or cremation

The choice between burial and cremation is another key factor in the cost of a funeral, with a significant impact on the funeral budget. Understanding the costs of each option helps you choose with full knowledge and makes the decision easier.

The price of a burial. For a burial, several costs have to be considered. The first is the coffin, then the purchase of a concession and a plot in a Geneva cemetery for 20 years, with the possibility of renewing it. These costs vary with the length of the concession and the location of the cemetery.

The cost of a cremation. For a cremation, the cost also depends on several factors. The choice of coffin and urn are the main ones. It is also worth knowing that crematoria in Switzerland do not accept cardboard coffins.

3. Breaking down the cost of a funeral in Geneva

In Geneva, the cost of a funeral depends on several elements and can differ from one funeral service to another. These include:

  • funeral supplies (coffins);
  • third-party services (crematorium, cemetery, death notices, etc.);
  • taxes related to the funeral;
  • floral arrangements;
  • a gathering after the funeral service (ceremony, reception, etc.).

For an accurate estimate, the simplest way is to contact a funeral service in the canton of Geneva directly: it knows the costs in detail and can help you understand them. Get my quote.

Planning a funeral involves many decisions and varied costs. It is therefore essential to know which expenses cannot be avoided.

Unavoidable costs

The first unavoidable expense is the coffin. In Switzerland, a fir coffin costs about 1,000 CHF. At Murith funeral services, the fir coffin is 940 CHF, but the price of a coffin varies with the type of wood and the quality: from 1,000 CHF to 10,000 CHF for the most elaborate models. Discover our coffin models.

Optional funeral services

On top of the unavoidable costs, optional services can increase the total: the services of a thanatopractor, the rental of a funeral home for the last farewell, the publication of death notices. These services vary from one company to another.

Murith funeral services have more than 135 years of experience in the funeral field and know-how passed down through five generations, from father to son.

Optional services during a funeral can also include:

  • an officiant, to personalise the farewell and pay tribute to the deceased;
  • musicians to play live music;
  • additional flowers to decorate the ceremony venue;
  • photography or video services to keep a record of important moments.

These additional services add a personal touch to the ceremony, but they also increase the total cost. To find out more, see how we organise funerals.

5. Floral arrangements for a funeral in Geneva

Floral arrangements are another expense to plan for. In Geneva, their price generally ranges between 100 CHF and 900 CHF.

They are an integral part of the funeral ceremony and can be personalised to your wishes. Whether it is a simple wreath or a more elaborate composition, they should be included in the funeral budget. Discover our floral arrangements in Geneva.

6. Funeral rooms

A funeral room is a place where the body of the deceased is kept before the funeral and burial. At Murith funeral services, our funeral rooms are open to families and their loved ones, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Discover our rooms and the Chapelle de la Cluse.

If the death occurs in a retirement home, a clinic or a hospital, the deceased is transferred to our premises by our service within hours.

7. In summary: the cost of a funeral in Geneva

Understanding the costs of a funeral helps you plan and manage expenses. Whether choosing between burial and cremation, or understanding the unavoidable costs and optional services, being well informed lets you make sound choices. To plan these costs ahead, you can also discover funeral pre-planning.
