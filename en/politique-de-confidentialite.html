Privacy policy

Which data we process, why, and how you stay in control of your information.

Who is responsible for your data?

A. Murith SA is committed to protecting the confidentiality of your personal data. The controller is A. Murith SA, funeral directors, 89 boulevard de la Cluse, 1205 Geneva, Switzerland (phone +41 22 809 56 00, e-mail murith@murith.ch). This policy applies to this website and follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Which data do we process?

  • When you visit the site: IP address, date and time, pages viewed, browser and device type. Our hosting provider records these technical data to keep the site running and secure.
  • When you contact us (contact and quote forms, e-mail, phone): first name, last name, e-mail address, phone number and the content of your message.

Please share only what is essential in the forms. More sensitive information (the situation of the deceased, wishes, religious beliefs) is discussed by phone or in a meeting.

Why do we use these data?

  • to answer your requests and call you back;
  • to prepare a quote and plan support for your family;
  • to keep the site secure and working;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

By sending a form, you agree that we use your data to handle your request. We do not sell them and do not use them for advertising. We pass them on to third parties only if they are trusted service providers bound by confidentiality, or when the law requires it or to prevent fraud or a threat.

Who receives your data?

Only the members of our team who need them have access. We also use technical providers, who must protect your data:

  • Vercel Inc.: website hosting (United States);
  • Resend: delivery of our form messages to our mailbox (United States);
  • embedded content: some pages show an OpenStreetMap map, an online catalogue (FlipBuilder) or a video (YouTube, no-cookie mode). When this content loads, your IP address is sent to these services, which apply their own rules.

Links to Google Maps, LinkedIn, Instagram or other sites take you away from our site: their own privacy policies then apply.

Your data abroad

Some providers (hosting, e-mail delivery) are based outside Switzerland, notably in the United States. Where the country does not offer a level of protection recognised as adequate, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses no advertising cookies and no tracking or analytics tool. It may save display settings and the progress of our checklists in your browser (local or session storage). This information stays on your device, does not identify you and is not used to track you. If we add an analytics tool, this page will be updated.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data as long as needed to handle your request and meet our legal obligations, then we delete or anonymise it. The hosting provider keeps technical logs for a limited period.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to the data is limited to staff who need it. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of or objection to its processing, and a copy in a common format. If you gave consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Write to us at murith@murith.ch or by post at 89 boulevard de la Cluse, 1205 Geneva; we may ask for proof of identity to handle your request.

You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC): www.edoeb.admin.ch.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add a service to the site. The online version prevails.
